Competitive Match Rules

1. Captain Score Submissions

Both Alpha and Bravo captains must submit match scores after game completion. If scores match, the match result is recorded instantly. In case of conflicting scores, moderators will review screenshot evidence.

2. Map Veto Regulations

Captains have 45 seconds per turn to ban maps. If a captain is AFK, a random ban will be automatically executed.

3. Fair Play & Anti-Cheat

Using third-party injectors, scripts, emulators with unfair macros, or toxic behavior results in an immediate permanent ban.
